Diversity Plaza 37th Road Annual Tree Lighting Ceremony

Diversity Plaza 37th Road Virtual Annual Tree Lighting Ceremony

RDRC Presents Friends of Diversity Plaza 37th Road, Virtual Annual Tree Lighting Ceremony in Jackson Heights, Saturday, December 12th 5pm, 37th Road and Roosevelt Avenue. 

Join Us Register Here

In RDRC’s efforts to collaborate, partner, and promote Queens Community Partners, Friends of Diversity Plaza 37th Road, recipient of the NYC Green Relief & Recovery Fund administered by City Parks Foundation, invites you to join them virtually in bringing in the Holidays with the Tree Lighting, Tidings of Good Cheer to all this Holiday Season.

Due to COVID-19, and to assure the safety of all, the ceremony will be Virtual, along with streaming live via Diversity’s social media sites on Facebook, and Instagram.

For more information on our partner Diversity Plaza 37th Road, connect with Friends of Diversity Plaza, and follow on Instagram @diversityplazanyc.
